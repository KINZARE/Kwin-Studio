# Kwin Studio Brand System V2 — Working Decision Record

Engagement mode: Refresh.

Category: Independent Digital Flagship Studio.
Primary audience: decision-makers at ambitious established companies, scale-ups and premium challenger brands.
Differentiating truth: Strategy and craft live in the same room.
Brand promise: We turn ambitious brands into unmistakable digital experiences.
Personality: intelligent, confident, selective, contemporary, precise, cultured, technically sophisticated, understated, human.

Creative direction: Editorial Precision / Spatial Signal.

Preserve: near-black foundation, controlled acid accent, editorial typography, scroll storytelling, layered spatial motion and experimental graphics.
Evolve: add warm off-white editorial moments, real interface proof, clearer commercial hierarchy, better route architecture and stronger trust/conversion.
Avoid: gaming/cyberpunk, generic AI, fake luxury, card-heavy SaaS patterns, excessive gradients/glow, decorative 3D, fake proof.

Color roles:
- Carbon / ink: primary backgrounds
- Warm off-white: editorial counter-environment
- Acid yellow-green: scarce highlight, focus and interaction signal
- Muted neutral: secondary copy
- Hairline neutral: borders/dividers

Typography:
- Grotesk-led editorial system
- Strong display hierarchy, readable body copy, fluid clamp scaling
- Keep family count minimal and weights disciplined

Imagery:
1. Real interface/work proof
2. Contextual imagery
3. Kwin studio graphics

Motion:
- Native scroll
- Transform/opacity first
- Early reveals
- Few high-value spatial moments
- Reduced-motion parity

Application rules:
- One dominant CTA per page
- Work is protagonist
- Accent is scarce
- Cards only for real grouping
- Mobile composition is intentional
- No invented clients, outcomes, metrics, testimonials, awards or team size
